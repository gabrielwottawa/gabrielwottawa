### 🧠 About Me

I'm a developer passionate about **clean architecture**, **best practices**, and **system integrations**.  
I have solid experience with **C# / .NET**, **Angular**, **TypeScript**, **PostgreSQL**, **SQL Server**, and **Redis**, mainly working with **distributed systems** and **microservices**.

</div>

- 🧩 Experience in **complex integrations** with external APIs and asynchronous messaging  
- ⚙️ Development of **performant and well-structured REST APIs**  
- 📡 Hands-on experience with **message queues and asynchronous communication patterns** using **RabbitMQ** and **Google Pub/Sub**  
- 🧠 Focused on **scalability**, **observability**, and **service resilience**  
- 🐳 Automation and environment orchestration using **Docker Compose**  
- 📦 Expertise in **domain organization and smart caching** with **Redis**
