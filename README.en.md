## About Me

I'm a developer passionate about **clean architecture**, **best practices**, and **system integrations**. I have experience with **C# / .NET**, **Angular**, **Node.js**, **TypeScript**, **PostgreSQL**, **SQL Server**, **MongoDB**, and **Redis**, primarily working with **distributed systems** and **microservices**.

## Experience & Focus

- Experience building **complex integrations** with external APIs and asynchronous messaging.
- Development of **high-performance, well-structured REST APIs**.
- Hands-on experience with **message queues and asynchronous communication patterns** using **RabbitMQ** and **Google Pub/Sub**.
- Focus on **scalability**, **observability**, and **service resilience**.
- Environment automation and orchestration with **Docker Compose**.
- **Domain organization and smart caching** with **Redis**.
